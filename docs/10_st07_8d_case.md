# ST07 8D-style Case Study

**D1/D2 - Team and problem:** Manufacturing Engineering, Quality and Maintenance review a sustained process-measurement drift at ST07 concentrated on M07B.

**D3 - Containment:** flag suspect production, verify gauge/calibration state, increase inspection of affected output.

**D4 - Root-cause evidence:** raw evidence implicated operator and machine. Adjustment materially weakened the operator explanation; M07B retained moderate association. Alternative explanations include variant mix, shift and maintenance state.

**D5/D6 - Corrective action:** reduce calibration interval, shift-start gauge verification, and tool-life interlock. The action is mechanism-consistent.

**D7 - Verification:** observed defect/process metrics improve post intervention, but event-study dynamics and placebo diagnostics prevent clean causal identification. Placebo-in-time p=0.600; placebo-in-space p=0.364.

**D8 - Learning:** preserve the action as operationally plausible but grade causal evidence **Inconclusive**. Do not equate post-period improvement with causal proof.
